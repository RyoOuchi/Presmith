From first thought to final slide

Three moves. One clear message.

  1. 01 / FRAME

    Find the point.

    Choose the one idea your audience should remember.

  2. 02 / SHOW

    Make it tangible.

    Use an example, a comparison, or a simple diagram.

  3. 03 / REFINE

    Remove the noise.

    Inspect every slide. Keep only what serves the story.

The layout is ready. The point of view is yours.
