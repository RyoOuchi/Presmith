06 / The review loop

Checks and visual judgment

The browser finds technical problems. Image review checks the communication.

Automated diagnostics

  • Manifest and duplicate IDs
  • Failed assets and JavaScript errors
  • Bounds, text overflow, and clipping
  • Warnings for text below 18px

Rendered image review

  • Readable hierarchy and spacing
  • Overlaps and diagram labels
  • Chart units and visual consistency
  • A clear narrative and accurate claims

The skill requires opening the images. Generating them is only the first step.

At most three check, render, inspect, and repair passes. Report anything unresolved.
